# Tranquil Fast launch promotion kit

Prepared 18 October 2026 (Europe/Zurich). Owner reports build 109 submitted to Apple; submission status was not independently re-read during marketing preparation. Instagram: **@tranquilfastapp**, owner-reported existing account with no posts. Other brand social accounts and paid budget are not confirmed. All content below is a draft; no post, message, account setup, subscription or advertising campaign has been published/started.

## Recommendation

Start with Instagram and the existing website, supported by the founder's existing personal network. Produce a small amount of useful, accurate material and learn which message gets real interest before buying automation. Use English and pounds for US-facing screen demonstrations, while showing that kilograms are also supported. Position the product as a calm fasting timer and weight tracker for adults, not a medical treatment or a personalized AI coach.

For the first two weeks, the proposed incremental software/ad budget is **$0**, pending the owner's actual budget choice. This is a recommendation, not an approved spending limit or a promised growth outcome. No new app build is needed for the proposed promotion work.

Day 0 means **manual release completed and public App Store download verified**, not submission or approval day. Until then, keep these as drafts.

Manageable cadence after the first week: two useful posts and one short demo per week, plus timely replies. Do not create an obligation to maintain every platform at once.

## Other platforms, in priority order

1. **Existing founder network:** a LinkedIn/personal-network launch post can explain the product honestly and invite feedback. It is a warm-start opportunity, not a prediction of consumer demand.
2. **Facebook Page:** sensible if it will be maintained alongside Instagram; native Business Suite can consolidate work. Do not create accounts or accept terms without the owner's requested setup flow.
3. **One extra short-video channel:** reuse a polished demo on YouTube Shorts or TikTok after Instagram shows which explanation is useful. Avoid making five separate content operations at launch.
4. **Relevant communities:** participate openly as the developer and follow each community's current promotion rules. Ask moderators where required. No automated link-dropping, mass messaging or concealed self-promotion.
5. **Product Hunt:** an optional later, prepared launch. Its audience is early adopters and makers; a bare submission is not a substitute for reaching fasting-app users. Prepare an honest product page and a real demo after the app is publicly usable. [Product Hunt's preparation guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch).
6. **Apple's own promotion tools:** use official App Store badges, short links and launch assets after public availability. [Apple promotion tools](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/promote/).

## Measurement and later paid tests

Use native Instagram insights where the account supports them, support themes and available App Store Connect download/acquisition/retention reports. Record results rather than relying on likes alone. App Store usage/retention data can be incomplete or withheld; absence of small-sample data is not proof that nobody returned.

Apple documents that campaign-link generation becomes available only after the app has analytics data; metrics have privacy thresholds. When available, create links inside ASC and use separate campaign names such as `instagram_bio`, `instagram_demo`, and `founder_launch`. Do not invent the provider token. Ordinary website UTM parameters alone do not prove App Store install attribution. [Apple campaign-link documentation](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect-analytics/acquisition/campaign-links).

Before a paid experiment: confirm a budget and stop date, a working public listing, useful initial feedback, a clear message and an actual install-measurement path. Compare a small Apple Ads search experiment with a Meta experiment on its merits; neither is being launched or funded now. A free app without current monetization needs a deliberate learning objective before paying to acquire users.
