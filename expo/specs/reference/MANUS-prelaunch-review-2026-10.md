# Tranquil Fast — Independent iOS Prelaunch Review

## 1. Executive summary

- **Scope/evidence:** Consolidated read-only static-review findings for clean source `expo`, branch `sdk-upgrade`, commit [`611528387dd483bd637e417b41b5ce19231cc834`](https://github.com/rod-andara/rork-tranquil-fast-coach/tree/611528387dd483bd637e417b41b5ce19231cc834/expo), plus current official policy evidence. The local branch and remote HEAD matched; pre-existing non-app worktree changes were left untouched.
- **Verdict: NO-GO.** Resolve the two source-proven critical defects, the high-priority state/consent defects, and the single consolidated release-artifact gate before App Review.
- **Top risk 1 — active-fast loss:** merely choosing another Fasting Plan can immediately discard an active fast without history or confirmation.
- **Top risk 2 — unintended/duplicated Health data:** the visible “Sync to Apple Health” switch is not a durable export opt-out, and startup/direct-write/sync operations can race.
- **Top risk 3 — unintended release surface:** `/_paywall` is a discovered Expo Router route; a signed build must also prove RevenueCat is disabled and no purchase surface is reachable.
- Pause currently counts paused time and resets its display; asynchronous notification scheduling can outlive cancellation, including after “Log Out.”
- App Privacy guidance is internally contradictory: local-only HealthKit processing is not itself off-device collection, while retained Sentry diagnostics are collected; final payload/linkage facts remain unverified.
- Build 104 working in TestFlight is **user-reported, not independently verified**. Stale repository statements that it was not built are not treated as contrary build evidence.
- No build, app run, simulator/device test, network-envelope inspection, EAS/App Store Connect/Sentry/private-backend inspection, credential access, or hosted-URL check was performed.

## 6. Open questions

1. **Fasting semantics:** Does Pause mean paused time is excluded from progress, completion, history, and averages? If not, the control should be renamed or removed rather than retaining its current implication.
2. **Plan-change outcome:** When a person changes plan during an active fast, should the session remain, be ended and recorded, or be deliberately discarded? What confirmation language makes that outcome unmistakable?
3. **Health data intent:** Does the Add Weight switch mean “write now” or “permit this entry ever to leave the app”? This decision determines A3 and the conservative treatment of legacy manual entries.
4. **Health history/deletion contract:** Is the promise all available history, ten years, or a recent trend window? After an app-created Health sample is deleted locally, should it reappear on import, stay locally suppressed, or eventually be removed from Health?
5. **Privacy decision ownership:** Can the business prove whether Sentry’s installation identifier is retained or ever re-linked, whether project-side IP prevention/scrubbing is active, and whether app-hang reporting is intentional? Those facts determine final Identifier/Linkage/App Privacy responses.
6. **Release ownership:** Which signed build is tied to the reviewed revision after fixes, and who will retain the build ID/fingerprint, resolved toolchain, RevenueCat-disabled configuration attestation, and A7 pass/fail matrix? The user-reported build 104 result cannot supply that evidence by itself.
7. **Submission ownership:** What are the final public Privacy/Support destinations, canonical launch name, current ASC Medical or Wellness answers, and screenshot families accepted by ASC for the submitted binary?
8. **Future monetization:** Does the no-user-identifying-egress invariant permit a RevenueCat pseudonymous ID and purchase history at all? If an exception is considered, who approves its precise limits before any flag is enabled?
