Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 May 2026

Tranquil Fast is operated by Rodrigo Andara, based in Switzerland, under the Andara Digital brand ("Tranquil Fast", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the Tranquil Fast mobile app and website handle information.

Summary

Tranquil Fast is designed to be privacy-first. The app works without an account, does not show ads, does not sell your data, and does not track you across apps or websites.

Your fasting and weight data are stored on your device. If you choose to use Apple Health sync, weight data can be read from and written to Apple Health on your device with your permission.

Information stored on your device

The app may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Fasting plans and fasting history
  • Weight entries and goal weight
  • App preferences, such as units, notification settings, and selected fasting plan
  • Your optional name or nickname, if you choose to provide one

This information is used for app functionality, such as showing your timer, charts, progress, and settings.

Apple Health

Apple Health sync is optional. If you enable it, Tranquil Fast requests permission to read and write weight data only. The app does not request access to blood glucose, heart rate, sleep, activity, nutrition, medical records, or other Apple Health data types.

You can manage or revoke Apple Health permissions at any time in the Apple Health app or iOS Settings.

Crash diagnostics

We use Sentry to receive limited crash diagnostics so we can fix bugs and improve app reliability. These diagnostics may include technical information such as app version, device model, iOS version, stack traces, and sanitized error metadata.

We configure crash diagnostics so that your weight values, goal weight, fasting history, Apple Health values, and optional name are not sent to Sentry.

Subscriptions and purchases

Tranquil Fast launches with core features free to use. Optional premium features may be introduced later. RevenueCat may be used in the future to manage subscriptions or purchases. In the current free version, RevenueCat is disabled at runtime and does not initialize or collect purchase-related information.

If premium features are introduced later, this Privacy Policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated as needed.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Tranquil Fast can schedule local fasting milestone reminders on your device. These reminders are local-only and do not require a remote push server. We do not collect a push token for the current version.

External links

The Learn section may link to external websites. These links open in Safari. External websites are operated by third parties and are subject to their own privacy policies and practices.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use third-party advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not require an account to use the app.
  • We do not transmit your fasting history, weight values, goal weight, or Apple Health values to our own server.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored locally by the app can be removed by deleting the app from your device. Apple Health data is managed separately in Apple Health and iOS Settings.

Children

Tranquil Fast is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. Intermittent fasting may not be suitable for minors. If you are under 18, use the app only with guidance from a parent, guardian, or qualified healthcare professional.

International users

Tranquil Fast is operated from Switzerland and may be used internationally. Limited crash diagnostics may be processed by our service provider Sentry according to their infrastructure and data-processing practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us at support@tranquilfast.app.
