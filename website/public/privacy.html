Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Tranquil Fast is operated by Rodrigo Andara, based in Switzerland, under the Andara Digital brand ("Tranquil Fast", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how the Tranquil Fast mobile app and website handle information.

At a glance

No account is required. We do not show ads, sell personal data, or use advertising SDKs to track you across apps or websites. Your fasting history, weight entries, goal weight, and preferences are stored locally by the app. Optional Apple Health integration is controlled by your permissions.

The app sends technical crash diagnostics to Sentry. This website uses Cloudflare for hosting and security. If you email us, we receive the information in your message. These services involve data processing beyond your device, as described below.

App information stored locally

The app stores your fasting plan and history, weight entries and goal weight, units, reminders, selected plan, and optional name or nickname on your device to provide the timer, charts, progress, and settings. You do not need to send this information to us to use those features. Your device and Apple backup settings may separately affect how local information is backed up.

Apple Health

Apple Health sync is optional. The app requests separate permissions to read and write weight. It does not request other Health data types. Read permission allows importing weights; writing permits exporting weights when you choose to do so. Health information is used for the app's weight-tracking features, not advertising.

You can change these permissions in Apple Health or iOS Settings. App records and Health records are separate: editing or deleting an app entry does not automatically edit or delete the corresponding Health record. See Apple Health help.

Crash diagnostics and Sentry

We use Sentry to investigate errors and improve reliability. Diagnostics can include app version, device model, operating-system version, timestamps, stack traces, error messages, and technical breadcrumbs about app interactions. They can also include automatically generated installation or device identifiers and approximate location derived from network information. Network requests to Sentry also expose a connection IP address, which may be processed for approximate location or retained according to the service configuration. These identifiers can associate events from the same installation; diagnostics should not be considered anonymous.

The app includes filtering intended to remove weight values, fasting history, goal weight, Apple Health values, and your optional name from diagnostics. Please do not send health information in bug reports unless it is necessary and you choose to share it. We do not use diagnostics for advertising.

Our Sentry organization uses the EU data-storage region. Error events on our current Developer plan are retained for 30 days under Sentry's published plan rules. Related records, such as issue summaries and diagnostic emails, may be retained separately. Sentry's global service operations and subprocessors may involve processing outside the EU. See Sentry's privacy information.

Website hosting and security

Cloudflare delivers and protects this website. Visiting it involves processing connection and request information such as your IP address, requested URL, browser information, and request time for delivery, reliability, and abuse prevention. Cloudflare operates a global network, so this information may be processed internationally. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

The website does not embed advertising, analytics, or Sentry scripts. Fonts are served from this website rather than requested from Google Fonts. If you choose a light or dark theme, your browser stores that preference under tf-theme in local storage. You can remove it by clearing this site's browser data. Cloudflare may use security cookies when needed to protect the site.

Support email

If you contact support@tranquilfast.app, we process your email address, message, and any attachments to answer your request and investigate problems. Our domain routes support email through Microsoft’s mail infrastructure. Please avoid sending passwords, verification codes, or unnecessary health information. Send your app version, iOS version, and device model when reporting a technical issue.

Support messages and diagnostic correspondence are kept for as long as needed to handle the request, follow up on a related problem, or meet applicable legal obligations. Hosting and security records follow the service provider's operational retention practices. We do not promise that deleting the app removes these separate records.

Notifications and purchases

Optional fasting reminders are scheduled locally on your device. The current app does not use a remote push server or collect a push token for those reminders. The current free version does not initialize RevenueCat for purchases. If we introduce paid features or change our data processing, we will update this policy and relevant App Store information.

External links

Learn resources and other external links take you to third-party services. Those services receive information when you visit them and have their own privacy practices. Their content and availability can change.

Your choices, access, and deletion

Use Settings → Erase All Data in the app to remove locally stored app records and reset preferences. Deleting the app also removes its local app data; offloading it retains app data. Apple Health records must be managed separately in Apple Health. Reconnecting Health and importing again can restore weights from Health to the app.

For information we hold through support or diagnostics, contact us to request access, correction, or deletion, or to raise an objection or privacy concern. Depending on the law that applies, you may also have rights to restrict processing or receive a copy of your information. We may need enough information to verify and locate your request. Because no app account is required, we may not be able to associate a diagnostic event with you from your name or email alone.

You can contact the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner at edoeb.admin.ch or your local data-protection authority if you have an unresolved concern. Mandatory rights under applicable law remain unaffected.

Purpose and international processing

We process information to provide the app and website, respond to support requests, keep services secure, and diagnose faults. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on providing the service you request and our legitimate interests in reliability, security, and support, as appropriate to the information and context. Optional Health access depends on your device permissions, which you can revoke.

We operate from Switzerland. Cloudflare, Sentry, and Microsoft may use infrastructure and subprocessors in other countries, including the United States. Where required, international transfers must be covered by an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate contractual safeguards. Contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to your request.

Age suitability

Tranquil Fast is designed for adults and is not directed to children. Intermittent fasting is not suitable for children or teenagers. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate.

Changes and contact

We will update the date above when this policy changes. For questions or privacy requests, contact Rodrigo Andara, operating Tranquil Fast under the Andara Digital brand in Switzerland, at support@tranquilfast.app.
