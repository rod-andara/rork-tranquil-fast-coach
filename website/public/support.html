Common topics

Apple Health: sync says it worked, but no weights appear

Open Health → your profile picture → Apps (or Apps and Services) and select Tranquil Fast. Check that reading Weight is allowed; write access alone cannot import existing weights. Labels vary by iOS version. Confirm that Health has weight records under Browse → Body Measurements → Weight → Show All Data, then return to Tranquil Fast and sync again.

Apple protects read permissions, so a completed request does not prove that reading was allowed. If no entries are imported, check permissions and existing Health data. Allow writing only if you want new manual entries exported, and use the export choice when saving an entry. Editing or deleting a record in one app does not automatically change the other.

Deleting your data

In Tranquil Fast, open Settings → Erase All Data and confirm to remove local app records and preferences. Deleting the app also removes local data; offloading retains it. Health records remain in Apple Health and must be deleted there separately. Importing from Health again can restore those weights to the app. For support email or diagnostic records, see our privacy and deletion information.

Notifications

Fasting milestone reminders are managed in the app under Settings → Notifications. You can also manage all notification permissions from iOS Settings → Tranquil Fast → Notifications.

Changing your fasting plan

Choose a plan from the app's Settings tab. Custom fasting durations range from 4 to 48 hours. These are timer options, not medical recommendations. If a fast is running, check the app's confirmation before changing your plan.

App crashing or not working

Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, email support@tranquilfast.app with the app version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, and steps that caused the issue. Do not erase data or reinstall as a first troubleshooting step: that can remove local history.
