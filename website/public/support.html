Common topics

Apple Health permissions

Go to the Health app → Browse → Body Measurements → Weight, then tap "Data Sources & Access" to manage Tranquil Fast's access. Or go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Tranquil Fast.

Deleting your data

All app data is stored locally on your device. To remove it, delete the Tranquil Fast app from your iPhone. Apple Health data is managed separately — you can delete it from the Health app.

Notifications

Fasting milestone reminders are managed in the app under Settings → Notifications. You can also manage all notification permissions from iOS Settings → Tranquil Fast → Notifications.

Changing your fasting plan

You can switch plans any time from the app's Settings tab. Custom windows let you set any fasting duration you prefer.

App crashing or not working

Try force-quitting the app and reopening it. If the issue persists, email us at support@tranquilfast.app with your iPhone model and iOS version — we'll investigate.
