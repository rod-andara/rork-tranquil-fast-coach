Questions

Common questions

Is Tranquil Fast really free?

Yes. Core tools — timer, fasting plans, weight logging, progress charts — are free and remain free. We may introduce optional premium features later, but the essentials will always be free.

Does it require an account?

No account, no sign-up, no email address. You open the app and use it. Your data stays on your device.

What fasting plans are supported?

14:10, 16:8 (most popular), 18:6, 20:4, OMAD, and a fully custom fasting window where you set your own start and duration.

How does Apple Health sync work?

Apple Health sync is completely optional. If you enable it, the app can read and write weight data only — no other health data types. You can revoke access any time in iOS Settings.

Is my weight data private?

Yes. Your weight, fasting history, and goal weight are stored on your device only. We never transmit this data to a server. Limited crash diagnostics (app version, device model, error traces) are collected to fix bugs — weight values are explicitly excluded from these.

Is this available on Android?

Tranquil Fast launches on iPhone first. Android may come later — no date set yet.
