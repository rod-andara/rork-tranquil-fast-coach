Questions

Common questions

Who is Tranquil Fast for?

Tranquil Fast is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Fasting is not suitable for everyone. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional before starting; see our health guidance. The app does not provide medical advice.

Is Tranquil Fast really free?

Core tools — timer, fasting plans, weight logging, and progress charts — will be free at launch. Optional premium features may be introduced later; any prices and purchase terms will be shown before purchase.

Does it require an account?

No account, no sign-up, no email address. You open the app and use it. Your fasting history and weight entries are stored on your device. See our privacy policy for crash diagnostics and website information.

What fasting plans are supported?

14:10, 16:8 (most popular), 18:6, 20:4, OMAD, and a custom fasting duration between 4 and 48 hours. Available plans are timer settings, not recommendations about how long you should fast.

How does Apple Health sync work?

Apple Health sync is optional and requests separate read and write permissions for weight. Allow reading to import existing weights; write permission alone does not allow imports. See our Apple Health help.

Is my weight data private?

Your weight entries, fasting history, and goal weight are stored locally. Optional Apple Health access is controlled by you. The app also sends technical crash diagnostics to Sentry; these may include installation identifiers and approximate location. See the privacy policy for details.

Is this available on Android?

Tranquil Fast launches on iPhone first. Android may come later — no date set yet.
